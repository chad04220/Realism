## Realism 3.0.0 — everything is a voxel

Matter is never created or destroyed. Every voxel stays a voxel and only changes form, and everything you see is a voxel.

### Matter only changes form
- **Fire:** a voxel that burns out becomes hot smoke, and some of it becomes ash. The flame is that smoke, glowing until it cools to grey. A burning tree turns into a column of smoke that rises and drifts on the breeze.
- **Water:** boiling turns water into steam voxels, and steam condenses back into water as it cools. Water thrown onto a fire flashes into steam.
- **Soot:** cool smoke that touches a surface slowly settles out of the air as soot (ash) and falls.
- **Explosions:** a charge's own voxels become its white-hot fireball. Everything a blast breaks is thrown as fragments that land somewhere; nothing is vaporised into nothing.
- **Rockets** burn their propellant voxels away as exhaust, getting lighter as they go.
- **Cracks and chiselling** break voxels off as fragments instead of deleting them.
- **Smoke and steam are real voxels.** They are carried by the simulated air, rise while hot, mix as they wander, and are stopped by walls: a fire indoors fills the room and spills out of the door.
- The only way out is through the open sides or top of the world, and that is counted. **F3** shows the total matter and how much has left. A new self-test checks the total stays exact through fire, boiling and a blast.

### Everything is a voxel
- **No smoothing:** surfaces are exactly the simulation's voxel faces. The smooth-surface mode and the **V** key are gone.
- **No particle effects:** fire, smoke, steam, dust and sparks are no longer sprites. Smoke and steam are semi-transparent voxels that absorb and scatter light over exactly the length of view ray inside each one, and hot gas glows by its temperature.
- **Per-voxel lighting:** shadows fall in whole voxels, and every corner is darkened by the voxels around it.
- **Fragments in flight** are drawn on the grid, where a voxel there would be.
- **No screen effects:** the heat-haze shimmer, the volumetric smoke pass and edge smoothing are gone.
- **Light only:** a blast's flash and the blowtorch flame are light, not matter.

### Under the hood
- The gas layer is stepped in parallel, and cool, drifting smoke is updated less often.
- Saves use format 2, which stores gas. Saves from 2.x still load, without gas.
- Video memory for the default world dropped to about 700 MB (1.3 GB at 0.02 m).
- New script command `ignite x y z radius fuel`.

### Install
Download `Realism-3.0.0-windows-x64.zip`, unzip it anywhere and run `Realism.exe`. Needs a GPU with OpenGL 4.3.

### Known issues
- A big fire makes a lot of smoke (a burning tree is well over 100,000 smoke voxels), and moving it costs CPU time.
- Smoke is trapped inside dense foliage until the leaves around it burn through.
- The hose and the workshop still add and remove matter: they are the player's tools, not the simulation.
- Rigid bodies aren't stress-checked yet.
