## Realism 3.0.1: fire stays where it is

One fire no longer sets the whole yard alight.

### Fixes
- **Radiant heat now falls off with distance.** Heat radiated by a fire spreads over a wider patch the further it travels (with the square of the distance) and fades through the air. A fire scorches what is near it instead of lighting single leaves and grass blades metres away.
- **Fires no longer create energy.** A hot surface could radiate more heat than it actually lost, so fires kept heating themselves until the campfire's embers hit the 3,600 °C limit, radiating megawatts. Now a surface radiates exactly what it loses: the yard's two fires give off about 150 kW in total, where before it climbed to tens of megawatts.
- **Green turf and leaves are wet.** Live grass is 60% water and leaves 50%, and that water has to boil off before they can burn. A lawn scorches at the edge of a fire instead of carrying a grass fire across the yard. A blowtorch or a big fire still dries them and sets them alight.
- A new self-test burns the yard's campfire and stove for 40 seconds and checks that nothing more than 1.5 m from either catches fire.

### Install
Download `Realism-3.0.1-windows-x64.zip`, unzip it anywhere and run `Realism.exe`. Needs a GPU with OpenGL 4.3.
