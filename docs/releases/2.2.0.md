## Realism 2.2.0 — nothing floats

Every voxel now has to be held up by the voxels around it, and water is made of voxels like everything else.

### Structure
- **Every voxel is held up only by its neighbours**: what it rests on, what it is bonded to beside it, or what it hangs from. Anything with nothing holding it falls.
- **Bonds have real strengths.** Inside a part, the material's own strength (soil ~15 kPa, brick 3 MPa, oak 50 MPa, steel 400 MPa); between parts, the joint (mortar, nails, screws, glue, bolts, welds). Loose rubble and parts that only rest can't hang off anything.
- **Load finds the stiffest way to the ground**, shared between all its supports; sideways bonds are checked as beams in bending and shear, hanging ones in tension. When a section is overloaded the load first tries another way round; whatever is left without support breaks off and falls, and the collapse can cascade.
- Undercut a soil bank or dig a tunnel too wide and it caves in. Saw into a branch and it snaps when the rest can't hold it. A brick leaning against a wall falls; a glued one stays.
- Your own blows, blasts, chiselling and building are checked straight away; slow changes such as fire are checked a few times a second.

### Water
- **Whole voxels.** Water free-falls when there's nothing under it, pours over edges, spreads and levels, and stands at the same height in connected containers.
- Holes below the surface jet at Torricelli's speed, √(2gh), at the rate the hole passes.
- **Exactly conserved**, voxel for voxel: water only leaves the world by boiling.
- **Drawn as voxels**, like glass: no shader surface, no fake waves.

### The scene, rebuilt to stand up
- The shed roof now has pine roof boards on the rafters, with the tiles resting on them; rafter tails run out to the eave; gutters sit on brackets; the verges have barge boards; the stove flashing lies on the roof.
- The tree's wood runs up into its crown; the water tower's braces and valve wheel are properly joined; the stove's logs lie on the embers.

### Fixes
- A very light voxel (foliage, bulb glass) next to a fire could cool past absolute zero in one step and send temperatures to infinity; cooling is now bounded by the voxel's surroundings.
- Saves from 2.1 load: fractional water is rounded to whole voxels, keeping the total.

### Install
Download `Realism-2.2.0-windows-x64.zip`, unzip it anywhere and run `Realism.exe`. Needs a GPU with OpenGL 4.3.

### Known issues
- Rigid bodies (loose objects) aren't stress-checked yet, and a heavy object resting on a structure doesn't load it.
- Compression never fails and there is no buckling.
- The player walks through loose objects.
